The decline into the base falls outside the range and never counts. Base high = its highest weekly high; "vs base high" is the close against it (green = above it by at most 15%). Base range = its high/low range (tight is good). Holding now = the latest daily close is still above the base high. Daily = close above 50d, 50d above 150d, 150d above 200d, 200d rising over a month (+ holds, - fails); green when the first two hold with the 50d rising. Vol x = transition-week volume vs the prior 10-week average. Hover a score for its parts.

ADR = 20-day average daily range (high/low), with the close's distance above the base high in ADRs in brackets: extension measured in the stock's own units. Confirmation requires at most 3 ADRs above the base high; the A+ grade at most 1.5. Under about 2.5% the stock is slow and a 13-week target takes time.

Earnings = latest filed quarter's net income vs a year earlier (SEC filings, point in time; S&P 500 coverage only, blank elsewhere); "accel" = at least +25% and faster than the quarter before (CANSLIM's C). Stop = 2% under the base high, never more than 8% below the close; the risk in brackets is entry-to-stop.
