Base = the trailing price range before the week Stage 2 began: the longest window whose high/low range stays within 35%, bounded by the weeks spent outside Stage 2 (a one-week dip cannot make a base). The decline into the base falls outside the range and never counts. Base high = its highest weekly high; "vs base high" is the close against it (green = above it by at most 15%). Base range = its high/low range (tight is good). Holding now = the latest daily close is still above the base high. Daily = close above 50d, 50d above 150d, 150d above 200d, 200d rising over a month (+ holds, - fails); green when the first two hold with the 50d rising. Vol x = transition-week volume vs the prior 10-week average. Hover a score for its parts.

ADR = 20-day average daily range (high/low), the stock's speed: under 2% is slow (a 13-week target takes time), 3% and up is a swing stock. Ext base (ADR) = how far the close sits above the base high, counted in ADRs: how late the entry is. 0 to 1 is the entry zone, up to 1.5 is still acceptable on the breakout, over 3 fails confirmation. Ext 10 EMA (ADR) = the close's distance from the 10-day EMA in ADRs; over 2 to 3 is extended on the daily and a place to sell into, not buy.

QS pct = the quant system's five-factor composite (trend, RS, volume, volatility, momentum; frozen weights), as a percentile of the S&P 1500 pool. Context only: as a ranking key it did not beat the plan on a two-year replay.

Earnings = latest filed quarter's net income vs a year earlier (SEC filings, point in time; S&P 500 coverage only, blank elsewhere); "accel" = at least +25% and faster than the quarter before (CANSLIM's C). Stop = 2% under the base high, never more than 8% below the close; the risk in brackets is entry-to-stop.
