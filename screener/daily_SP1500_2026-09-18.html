How to read this

Green is constructive for equities, red is not. On the rates block that inverts: falling yields print green because they are a tailwind.

Leaders are the strongest liquid names inside the three sectors with the best three-month relative strength, filtered to those above their own 50-day line. It is a reading list, not a buy list. The pre-registered studies behind this project found no forward edge in selection, and the weekly plan remains the only place trades are proposed. The regime and breadth blocks are the parts with evidence behind them.

Sector member breadth covers 619 mapped tickers, fewer than the full universe, so those percentages describe the mapped subset only.
